What this does and does not mean
--------------------------------
  * **Every number already recorded against "432 modules" stays correct.** It
    was measured on the target as it stood, and `benchmarks/results/**` is
    never rewritten. The denominator belongs to the measurement, not to today.
  * **A number measured from here on has a denominator of 422**, and putting
    one next to a 432-era number is the "do not mix conditions" mistake with a
    different variable. Say which the number is against.
  * `tools/ledger-reference.sh` and anything else driving off
    `it-modules.txt` is now extracting from a list that names ten modules with
    no source. That is not a failure today (the oleans are there), but it is a
    reference tree nobody could regenerate from the sources.

Not investigated: whether the ten were deleted, renamed, or moved behind a
different library root, and whether the target's git history says so. The
target is not this repository's to change.
