THE FIX
-------
PrintKey.ownKey hashes, for an inductive, the name and type of every member the page shows
(memberConsts: the constructor, the structure and its parents with their projections, and every
flattened field's projection), instead of the constructors' types and the field names. This is the
member set the exact key (keyOf) already reads.

  minus Ergodic and QuasiErgodic, nothing else moved.

  v4.33.1's keys with both readers and comparing each to its head: equal keys 1,380 -> 1,377 of 1,687

  (it was not among the 62): a member's type differs between the versions as a term, not on the page.
