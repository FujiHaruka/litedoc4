THE COMPARATOR (tools/lib/reader-compare.py --classify)
  The exact mode (the gates' 10 arguments) is unchanged: on the five S versions it printed the
  same lines as tools/mv-reader-gate.sh's reader-equals-native items, exit 0.
  --classify takes the same 10 arguments after `--classify <report.json>`, applies the same
  rename / contentHash / identity rules, and prints the printed-field list it uses (verbatim
  in every classification below). Causes are tried in order, cumulatively, on both texts; the
  first after which they are equal names the bucket. Whitespace, hygiene, coercion arrows, `@`
  and namespace qualification are the pre-registered rules of
  benchmarks/results/mathlib-printer-drift-2026-10-04.txt; set-builder and `↧` are its F1 and
  F2. The structure-instance step was added after the v4.32.2 trial run, from its one unrecognised
  example, before the full run.
  The set-builder step was widened after the trial: it first turned `setOf fun x => p` back
  into `{x | p}` only inside parentheses, which left 65 field occurrences in 32 declarations
  "not recognised" (every one an old `setOf` whose `fun` forced new parentheses or ended at a
  `,`); it now rewrites `setOf fun x =>` to `{x |` and compares with every bracket removed,
  and only when the reader's text has `setOf fun` and the native one does not.

  Sample first (S, tools/mv-reader-gate.sh's five reader-filled versions against their native
  entries, the work area kept and deleted afterwards): 0 defects in each of v1..v5, 0 printed
  differences, the rename applied 6 times per version in 6 declarations.
  Falsified once each, on a copy of S v5's reader IR (run with the comparator before its last
  three edits: the widened set-builder step, the structure-instance step, and defects recorded
  per declaration for the site trace; none changes what is a defect or what is not recognised):
    planted `"line":56` -> 57 in Example.Attrs.zero (non-printed): exit 1,
      defects 1: Example.Attrs Example.Attrs.zero: `line` native 56 reader 57
    planted `"type":"Nat"` -> "Nat0" in the same declaration (printed): exit 0, defects 0,
      cause not recognised 1: Example.Attrs Example.Attrs.zero type, native 'Nat' reader 'Nat0'
  The site trace failed once before it passed: on S, before the comparator recorded the
  reducibility rename per declaration, it reported 15 untraced pages (3 per version, the
  instances whose `attrs` render the running Lean's spelling); on the v4.32.2 trial it reported
  3 (eqOmitted not mapped to equations; `names` entries tied to refs rather than to the
  rendered code; the defect's page). Each was then named, not excused: the rename and the
  defect are recorded per declaration and a page traced to a defect is counted as such.
