Mathlib itself as the documented package, after the sortUtf16 fix — and the 103
====================================================================================
2026-10-03

What this answers
-----------------
1. How long does one `litedoc4 build --lib Mathlib` take now that `sortUtf16`
   merge-sorts (ba2bd30)? mathlib-render-2026-10-03.txt measured 4,995.7 s with the
   quadratic sort and extrapolated ≈ 483 s without it.
2. That file's render line read `declarations 298656/311415 (12862 suppressed)`, and
   298,656 + 12,862 = 311,518 is 103 more than the 311,415 declarations in the IR.
   Why?

Answers:
1. 449.9 s (measured, single run), of which the global step is 30.4 s.
2. A counting artefact, not a rendering defect. "suppressed" is the size of the
   site-wide set of *member names* (constructors, fields, parent projections listed
   under some structure or inductive), not a count of IR declarations. 103 of those
   member names are not IR declarations at all: 51 inherited fields whose projection
   lives in a dependency (50 in Lean core, 1 in Batteries), and 52 private
   constructors or fields of Mathlib structures, which the extractor drops as
   internal names. No declaration is rendered twice and no page carries a name it
   should not.

3. The 103
----------
What the render line counts (src/Litedoc4/Render/Site.lean, renderSite; Page.lean,
suppressedOf):
  declarationsInIr       Σ over modules of the module's declarations      311,415
  declarationsSuppressed |S|, S = every member name of every declaration     12,862
  declarationsRendered   declarations whose name is not in S                298,656
So rendered + suppressed − in-IR = |S \ names of IR declarations|, provided no name
is declared twice in the IR. Measured on the IR of this run:
  declarations in IR 311,415, distinct names 311,415, duplicated names 0
  S ∩ IR names 12,759 (= 311,415 − 298,656), S \ IR names 103

The 103, classified by the member entries that name them (751 + 51 + 1 = 803):
  51 names   label field, isDirect false (inherited), 751 entries in Mathlib
             structures. Each one's defining module, from this run's link index:
             50 in Lean core (Init.*), 1 in Batteries (RatCast.ratCast,
             Batteries.Classes.RatCast). Examples: Zero.zero (87 owners), Mul.mul
             (85), Add.add (81), One.one (66), Setoid.iseqv and Setoid.r (8 each),
             Std.Refl.refl (4), Lean.Meta.Rewrite.Config.occs (1,
             Mathlib.Tactic.GRewrite.Config)
  52 names   `_private.Mathlib.<module>.0.…` — 51 private constructors (label ctor:
             `structure … where private mk ::`; 50 end in `.mk`, mostly
             `<Cat>.Hom.mk` / `<Cat>.mk`, and 1 is TopModuleCat.Hom.ofHom'. By
             module area: Algebra.Category 28 names, Order.Category 13,
             RepresentationTheory 4, Topology 3, CategoryTheory 2,
             Algebra.Module 2 (the ctor and field below)) and 1 private field
             (Module.IsStablyFree.exist_free_prod', label field, direct). Each is a
             member of exactly one structure, in the module its private prefix
             names
  0 names    a public Mathlib declaration the extractor dropped

Why the 52 private names are not IR declarations: extractor/Extract.lean's
isBlackListed drops `Name.isInternal` names (doc-gen4's rule), and a `_private.…`
name is internal. Its isProjFn exception does not rescue the private field, because
the field's prefix `_private.….0.Module.IsStablyFree` is not a structure. A probe in
the clone's environment confirmed, for exist_free_prod', IsStablyFree.mk and
GrpCat.Hom.mk: present in the module's constNames, `env.find?` some, isInternal true;
exist_free_prod' is a projection function, the two constructors are not.

What the site does with the 103 (a scan of the `id="…"` attributes of all 8,174
html files, plus declarations/name-map.json):
  - the 51 inherited dependency fields: 0 appear as an id anywhere. They are
    rendered as `<li class="field inherited">` linking to the dependency, as
    e2e/micro's Example.Preferred does with Inhabited.default. 24 of them are keys
    in name-map.json — as dependency names (3,414 such entries), which is how they
    are linked; the search index does not carry them
  - the 52 private names: each appears as an id exactly once, on its owner's page,
    and nowhere else. For a constructor it is the id of the structure's
    `<ul class="fields">` (the anchor every structure's constructor gets), for the
    private field the field's `<li>`. None is a name-map key or in the search index
site-gate checked 311,415 search-index entries and the IR holds exactly 311,415
declarations, so the index is the IR's declarations and none of the 103 is in it.
"search-index → pages 311,415" says every one of them resolves to an anchor: 298,656
as a declaration block ("pages → search-index"), the other 12,759 as member anchors
on their owners' pages.

Reproduced cheaply first: tools/e2e-micro.sh prints
`declarations 56/97 (42 suppressed)`, 56 + 42 = 98, a gap of 1, and that one name is
Inhabited.default, the field Example.Preferred inherits from Lean core (measured,
same analysis, HEAD ba2bd30).

Verdict: a counting artefact. The 12,862 is not "declarations suppressed" but
"distinct member names", and 103 of those were never declarations of this IR. If the
three numbers are meant to reconcile, the one-line change is to report suppressed
as the IR declarations whose name is in S — equivalently in-IR minus rendered
(12,759 here) — instead of |S|. Not applied.
