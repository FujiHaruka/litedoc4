How Lean 4 spells the characters `fixtures/md/*-expected.json` contains
(measured 2026-09-02)

WHY
  `tools/gen-md-oracle-cases.py` writes those two corpora into
  `test/Litedoc4Test/MdOracleCases.lean` as Lean string literals. The corpora
  hold NUL, TAB, CR, LF and astral characters (U+1D49C, U+1F600), and
  `tools/gen-assets.py` -- the precedent -- deliberately refuses everything but
  \ and " , saying "Lean's \x and \u escapes have not been measured here".
  This is that measurement.

CONDITIONS
  hardware        Apple M1, macOS 26.6.2, arm64
  Lean            leanprover/lean4:v4.31.0 (the toolchain `e2e/micro` pins)
                  Lean (version 4.31.0, arm64-apple-darwin24.6.0,
                  commit 68218e876d2a38b1985b8590fff244a83c321783, Release)
  how             one `.lean` file per spelling, run with `lake env lean --run`
                  from `.lake/host`, printing `(String.toUTF8 <literal>).toList`
  python          3.9.6 (the side that writes the literals)

  "astral surrogates" is `"\ud83d\ude00"`, the way JSON spells U+1F600. It is
  the one line here that matters more than the rest: Lean **accepts** it,
  exits 0, and hands back two NUL bytes. A generator that reached for the JSON
  spelling would produce a corpus that is silently not the corpus, with nothing
  in the build saying so. `gen-md-oracle-cases.py` refuses a surrogate for this
  reason and writes astral characters out raw, which is the row above it.

  `b` and `abc` after the escape are hex digits and were not eaten, so the
  corpus's `a<NUL>b` and `<CR>abc` can be written with `\xHH` and nothing else.
