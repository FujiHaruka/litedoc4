## 2. The wall clock moved 3x between two runs of the same command (measured)

  full 1   26.32 s   peak RSS 3.22 GB
  full 2    9.12 s   peak RSS 3.20 GB
  full 3    8.85 s   peak RSS 3.23 GB
  full 4    8.85 s   peak RSS 3.21 GB
  full 5    8.80 s   peak RSS 3.23 GB

  no-op    0.26 / 0.25 / 0.25 / 0.25 / 0.25 s

Every one of the five built the site from nothing and did exactly the work the
integers above describe. What separates run 1 from runs 2-5 is the page cache:
the extraction is ~96% of a full build and is dominated by `importModules`, and
the oleans are mmap'ed. Run 1 paged Mathlib in; the rest read it from memory.

**README's 24.5 s is not rewritten.** It is the first-run figure, which is the one
a CI job pays, and 26.32 s here reproduces it (+7%, one run against a median of
six). The 8.8 s is a second full build in the same session — a real number for a
real state, and the wrong one to put next to a CI cost.

The no-op's 0.25 s sits just under the recorded 0.31 s; one run of five does not
overwrite a median, and the integers say the work is identical.
