L through the reader (docs/multiversion/implementation.md, Item 6 plan, step 9), predicted before it ran
======================================================================================================

Written 2026-10-10, before .github/workflows/mv-l.yml ran this arm. Every figure here is (theoretical)
unless labelled otherwise: measured parts composed per row, unverified until the run. The run's own log
is a separate file; this one is not edited after the run.

What runs: ubuntu-latest (4 vCPU = 2 cores, 16 GB + 3 GB swap), benchmarks/tools/mv-l-run.sh. The root
is a clone of Mathlib at v4.34.1; `litedoc4 build --root <clone> --lib Mathlib --versions
v4.32.2,v4.33.0,v4.33.1,v4.34.1 --jobs 4`, litedoc4.toml `no_equations_under = ["Mathlib.Tactic",
"Mathlib.Meta"]`. litedoc4 built on e2e/micro's v4.31.0; the reader on v4.34.1 at -O3; the native
extractor at -O0 (unchanged since step 4; the reuse seam passes nothing natively).
  (a) empty --out, --reader-check: v4.34.1 extracted natively, then one reader session reads the
      three older ones (round 1 built whole, rounds 2 and 3 patched), check-patch and check-keys on.
  (b) a copy of (a)'s --out, `litedoc4 store remove` of the three older entries, --reader-alone.
  compare: record.json and entry.pack.gz of the three, (a) against (b), byte for byte.

(4) Session against read-alone. r1 is predicted slower than alone by 250-420 s (the hash, the key
  pass and the checks, less alone's newest import and manual-root ask), as on M. r3 without its
  check phases is predicted at 276-492 s against alone's 325-634 s for v4.33.1: on L, unlike M, the
  patch pair is where the session is predicted to break even or win, because reuse saves
  extraction (x14.5 from M) while the hash grows only x3.6-4.9.
  This is the prediction most in the plan's favour; it rests on extraction scaling by declarations.
  Falsified: r3 minus check-patch and check-keys >= alone's `v4.33.1 read` phase.

(5) Memory. Peaks are sequential (the session stops in `finally` before the render), not additive.
  Naive linear scaling of M's session peak (4,840 MiB M1 RSS) gives 17.2-23.8 GiB, which does not fit.
  Not taken: M's RSS counts the newest oleans' file pages, and PL ran the same five-round session over
  all of Mathlib at a peak footprint of 10.08 GB (9.39 GiB) on the M1, without check modes.
  Session anon peak 9.5-12.5 GiB (RN's one-version hybrid anon 7.34 GiB x the session's 1.3-1.7 over
  read-alone on M; PL's 9.39 GiB plus the check modes' second state); RSS incl. file pages up to what
  the machine holds (~13-15 GiB). Predicted to fit 16 GB + 3 GB swap, with 1.5-5 GiB MemAvailable at
  the minimum: the upper end leaves about 2 GiB. If it does not, the kernel kills the reader, the
  session's stdout ends, litedoc4 says `the reader session exited <code> while reading version ...`
  and (a) exits 1; (b) still runs, since v4.34.1 is put before the session starts, and the end step
  writes the kernel's OOM lines to oom.txt. (a) can also end at its 130-min step limit with the
  session mid-round; (b) then kills that reader before it starts.
  Read-alone (b) anon 6-8 GiB (RN 7.34). Render of four versions 5.4 GiB peak (RM: 3.67 + 0.585 x 3,
  extrapolated; litedoc4 links mimalloc 2.2.3 through v4.31.0).
  Falsified: session anon peak outside 9.5-12.5 GiB, or any OOM line.
