(4) Why 419 of 438 page files change in a minor release (measured, v4.32.2 -> v4.33.0)
  228 modules keep their content file and still get a new page file. In 211 of them the only
  difference is dependency line ranges: same name, same root, same module, other lines (1,714
  entries under Init, 144 under Lean, 3 under Batteries; e.g. `Add`: Init.Prelude 1514-1517 ->
  1517-1520). Lean core moves in every Mathlib minor release, and every page that names a core
  declaration carries its lines. Of the other 17, 14 also change their own `lines` (one of
  them its imports too) and 3 which names they carry (2 entries added or removed, 1 moved to
  another core module).
  The lever: dependency line ranges in one per-version table, a page naming only the declaration.
  It moves bytes from every page into one file every page fetches; step 3 weighs that, since it
  decides what a page view fetches. At Mathlib it is up to the page files' 5.2 MB per minor
  release in (6) (extrapolated).
