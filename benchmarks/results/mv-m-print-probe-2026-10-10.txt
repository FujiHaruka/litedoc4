Verdict A: equations are the one feature that costs a lot (-21% of the process, and the only one
whose removal leaves a renderable IR worth discussing); tagged code is cheap and cannot be
dropped without the renderer refusing the IR; refs cost nothing measurable. All three together
give -25 to -27%, far from 2x: signature printing alone is 10 s and the phases before extraction are
11.7 s.

ESTIMATE (theoretical; unverified) — per-version process time on M at --jobs 1 if only the
declarations whose key differs, and the new ones, were printed
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Premises:
  P1 the phases before extraction stay as measured: a1 cycles 2-3 total minus extract = 11.7 s
     (decode 7.2, check ilean 1.3, merge 1.1, finalize 0.9, newest import 0.4, the rest 0.8)
     (measured)
  P2 the extract phase outside analyze stays: 1.09 s (link index, writeIR, sorry) (measured)
  P3 a reused declaration still pays analyze's non-printing part; for all declarations that is
     16.49 - 9.97 - 5.96 = 0.56 s (measured), kept whole
  P4 printing (signature + equations, 15.93 s) scales with the profiled share of the declarations
     to print (measured shares above; the share is of all analyze time, a slight overcount)
  P5 the key costs 0.85 s (measured, both hashes and docstrings for every candidate)
  P6 reading the adjacent version's IR into the extractor costs 0.5 s (assumed; nothing measured)
  P7 no exactness check runs, and the adjacent version's IR already exists
Builtin key: 11.7 + 1.09 + 0.56 + share x 15.93 + 0.85 + 0.5
  v4.32.2 <- v4.33.0   15.3 s   (1.9x against a1's 29.3 s of cycles 2-3)
  v4.33.0 <- v4.33.1   14.7 s   (2.0x)
  v4.33.1 <- v4.34.1   16.2 s   (1.8x)
Full key: 15.5 / 14.7 / 17.5 s.
Combined with dropping --equations (the most expensive feature): it removes only the printed
share's equations (share x about 6.3 s), 15.1 / 14.7 / 15.6 s with the builtin key. Dropping
--equations alone: 23.0-24.8 s (measured, a2), 1.25-1.27x paired by cycle.
Floor: with nothing printed, 11.7 + 1.09 + 0.56 + 0.85 + 0.5 = 14.7 s, 2.0x. So 2x through
reuse is reachable only on a pair that barely changed; on the two real pairs the estimate lands
at 1.8-1.9x, and what blocks it is the 11.7 s before extraction (decode alone 7.2 s), not
printing.
