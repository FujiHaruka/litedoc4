L, E6 + E5 on the runner: v4.33.1 read alone against read with --reuse-from, all of Mathlib
==========================================================================================

E6 (inexact print reuse across processes) and E5 (read-alone's fixed costs) of
docs/multiversion/implementation.md ("Levers plan: beating read-alone on M"), on full Mathlib on
ubuntu-latest, after both won on M (benchmarks/results/mv-m-reuse-key-e5-2026-10-10.txt).
Workflow mv-l.yml, `benchmarks/tools/mv-l-run.sh reuse`. Two runs reached v4.33.1 and none got
further; the fourth was cancelled (decided 2026-10-10, user's call: full-Mathlib builds are
expensive, and what can be estimated is estimated until the more careful verification before
v2). v4.33.0 and v4.32.2 were never measured here.
