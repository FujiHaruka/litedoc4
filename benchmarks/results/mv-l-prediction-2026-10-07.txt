==========================================================================================

Written 2026-10-07, before .github/workflows/mv-l.yml first ran. Everything here is (theoretical):
a composition of the measured parts named per row, unverified until the run. The run's own log is a
separate file; this one is not edited after the run.

What runs: `litedoc4 build --root <Mathlib clone> --lib Mathlib --versions ... --jobs 4` on a
GitHub-hosted ubuntu-latest runner (4 vCPU = 2 cores, 16 GB), with `no_equations_under =
["Mathlib.Tactic", "Mathlib.Meta"]` (the flags of the M run and of the runner baseline):
  (a) v4.32.2, v4.33.0 from nothing; (b) v4.33.1 added; (c) all three again.
