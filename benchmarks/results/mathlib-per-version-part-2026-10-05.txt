How small the per-version part of a multi-version Mathlib site can be (plan U10)
================================================================================
2026-10-05

Question
--------
Declaration content is stored once, content-addressed (D5, decided 2026-10-05). The per-version
part then grows with every version: the manifest, own positions, dependency link targets, and the
global files (Used by, name map, search index, instances, module list). The U6 sizing stored each
of them whole for every version. A theoretical lower bound (plan U10) put 11 versions at about
54 MB and 51 at about 92 MB. It assumed that each per-version part changes in proportion to the
declarations that changed (about 3% per minor release). That was not measured for Used by or the
global files.
This run measures, on one minor pair (v4.32.2 -> v4.33.0) and one patch pair (v4.33.0 -> v4.33.1),
each part whole and as a delta against the previous version, and the content store's growth. It
then replaces the assumptions with these numbers for 11 and 51 versions, with and without Used by.

------

  2. The plan's lower bound is close. Shape (i) is 52.1 MB at 11 versions and 96.9 MB at 51,
     against the theoretical 54 and 92 MB. But the shape inside it is different. The content store is
     most of shape (i): 35.8 of 52.1 MB at 11 versions, 62.8 of 96.9 MB at 51. Of that, 29.1 MB is
     the base version.

  3. In shape (ii), Used by is the largest part: 116.0 of 293.0 MB at 11 versions (40%) and 537.7
     of 1,155.0 MB (47%) at 51, with gzip. Next are the other global files (54.6 / 253.1 MB) and
     the manifest (31.2 / 144.7 MB). The manifest does not compress: 338,809 8-byte hashes per
     version are 2.71 MB of random bytes. At 51 versions the manifests (144.7 MB) are larger than
     the whole content store (139.8 MB).

  4. Used by against the ≈3% assumption: it holds for this minor pair. Its byte delta is
     140,241 B, which is 3.37% of the whole file under zstd -19. As a semantic delta, 34,776
     (used, user) pairs changed (21,813 added, 12,963 removed) out of 1,915,412, which is 1.82%.
     That delta compresses to 164,824 B. For comparison, 2.95% of declarations had new content
     (U3). In the patch pair, Used by is byte-identical. The manifest (3.37%) and the
     relatively encoded positions (4.21%) also change at about the rate of the declarations.
     Two global files do not. search-index.bin's delta is 32.0% of the whole and modules.json's
     is 32.8%. In shape (i) at 51 versions, the search index deltas alone are 12.7 MB, more than
     all of Used by (7.65 MB).

  5. Surprises:
     - The encoding of positions decides their delta. The positions as absolute JSON give a zstd
       delta of 31.0% of the whole. The same positions relative to the previous item give 4.2%.
       The cause is that 107,166 of 330,497 items moved by a whole number of lines with their
       shape unchanged.
     - zstd --patch-from of a byte-identical 80 MB file is 27,713 B, not about 0. An identical
       file has to be stored as "same as the previous version", not as a patch.
     - The content store holds blobs in the IR's own form. That form is about 2x the page-data
       form U7 measured: 57.5 MB with gzip per module pack, against U7's 28.6 MB for every page
       of v4.31.0. Compressing each blob alone is 3.5x worse than compressing packs (202.6 MB).
     - The definition of a blob reproduces U3 exactly. 308,449 of v4.33.0's 317,834 declaration
       blobs are already in v4.32.2's store, and that is U3's level-(d) count. The two choices
       below change nothing in this pair: keeping the dependency target's name in the blob, and
       keeping every IR field.

Definitions
-----------
  Blob (part 1). One declaration's content, stored once and found by a hash of its bytes.
    The blob is the IR declaration record with `line`, `col`, `endLine`, `endCol`,
    `selectionRange` and `index` removed. In `attrs`, `implicit_reducible` is rewritten to
    `instance_reducible`. It is serialized as compact JSON (UTF-8, no spaces) in the IR's key
    order. The name stays in the blob, and the manifest holds only hashes.
    Every other IR field stays, `refs` included. The blob carries link targets as the IR does:
    a name per span, plus the module of each referenced name in `refs`. It carries no
    dependency revision and no line range, since the IR has neither (render adds them from
    link-index.lidx and lake-manifest.json). This is U3's level (d) plus the dependency
    target's name, which a renderer needs to find the line range. The cross-check above shows
    that this extra name changes no count in this pair.
    The stored v4.32.2 blob says `instance_reducible` where v4.32.2's page today says
    `implicit_reducible`. A renderer would print it per version, or accept that.
    Also content-addressed, as two more blob kinds: a module header (the IR module record
    without `declarations`, `moduleDocs` and `schemaVersion`, so: module, imports, tactics)
    and each module docstring's text.
    Hash: BLAKE2b, 8 bytes. A check over all three versions (692,551 distinct blobs, with and
    without refs) found no two distinct blobs with the same hash (sha256 of each compared).
    "Without Used by" variant (`norefs`): the declaration blob without `refs`. `refs` feeds Used
    by. Its other use, a dependency link's module, is also in part 4.
  Content store, measured three ways:
    per blob        each blob gzip -6 on its own (the wrong way for small blobs, given for scale)
    per module      the distinct blobs, grouped by the first module that holds them, joined with
                    "\n" and compressed per pack (gzip -6, brotli -q 11)
    one stream      all of a store or of a version-delta's new blobs as one stream (zstd -19
                    --long=31, xz -9e). For a delta, also zstd --patch-from the old store's
                    stream
  Manifest (part 2). For each module, in page order: its header's hash, then its module
    docstrings and declarations, sorted the way render's `pageItems` sorts them (line, col,
    sequence). All declarations, including any that render suppresses.
    manifest.json {module: [16-hex hash, ...]}. manifest.bin, per module: u16 name length,
    name, u32 count, 8-byte hashes. The binary form is obviously better, because hex doubles
    random bytes and no compressor recovers that.
  Own positions (part 3), in manifest order:
    positions.json      {module: [[line, col, endLine, endCol, [sl, sc, el, ec]] for a
                        declaration, [line, col] for a module docstring]}
    positions-rel.json  the same, each item relative: line - previous item's line, col,
                        endLine - line, endCol, selection range relative to the item's line
    shift-aware delta   (semantic) for each module of B, B's items in page order. An item
                        whose key (declaration name, or the docstring's text hash) exists in
                        A's same module with the same relative shape is covered by a run
                        [first index, count, line shift]. Every other item is written
                        explicitly
  Dependency link targets (part 4). {"rev": {root: "repo@revision"}, "names": {name: [module,
    startLine, endLine]}}. The names are every dependency declaration that some declaration of
    the version links to from a signature, equation or structure-member span, with the module
    from `refs` and the range from link-index.lidx. This is 3,441 / 3,439 names, not the
    lidx's 88-90 k. The revision is stored once per root (10 roots, 7 repositories).
  Global files (part 5). The ten files the site writes besides module pages
    (Global/Artifacts.lean `artifactFiles`): declarations/used-by.json,
    declarations/name-map.json, search-index.bin, instances.json, modules.json, index.html,
    404.html, search.html, foundational_types.html, references.html. Each as written, byte for
    byte. The assets (style.css, app.js, favicon.svg; 45,229 B raw) do not depend on the version
    and are counted once as files, not as bytes.
  Deltas.
    byte delta      zstd -19 --long=31 --patch-from=<A's file> <B's file>. Round trip
                    (zstd -d --patch-from) checked byte for byte on manifest.json and on
                    used-by.json in both pairs
    semantic delta  manifest: per changed module, opcodes [A index, A count, B hashes]
                    (difflib), plus removed modules. positions: the shift-aware delta above.
                    deplinks: changed revisions, removed names, and set names. Used by:
                    one line per (used, user) pair added or removed

6. What dominates
-----------------
  - Shape (i): the content store, 69% at 11 versions and 65% at 51. Of the per-version parts,
    the global files other than Used by come first at 51 versions, because of search-index.bin's
    32% deltas. Used by is next (7.65 MB), then the manifest (5.15 MB).
  - Shape (ii): Used by (40% / 47% with gzip), then the other global files, then the manifest.
    The manifest is incompressible: 2.71 MB of every version's 2.84 MB is hashes. The content
    store becomes a minority as versions grow: 25% of the total at 11 versions, 12% at 51.
  - Against the U6 sizing (hosting-and-client-render-2026-10-05.txt, row 3, gzip): its
    per-version global files (168 MB at 11) agree with this run's 170.6 MB (Used by + global
    files). Its shared content (33 MB at 11) is low by 2.2x, because the blobs here are IR
    records and new content is packed per module per transition (74.0 MB). Its 119 MB of page
    shells is replaced here by manifest + positions, 47.9 MB.

7. What is not measured
-----------------------
  - A compact blob encoding. Blobs are the IR record. U7's page-data scheme was 0.50x of this
    run's per-module packs for the same Mathlib scale (28.6 MB against 57.5 MB with gzip).
    v4.31.0 against v4.32.2 is not the same input, so this is (theoretical) for the content
    store, and it does not touch the per-version parts.
  - Anything but these two pairs. The 11 / 51 totals assume every minor transition is like
    v4.32.2 -> v4.33.0 and every patch transition like a toolchain-only bump.
  - D10's one printer, U9's hybrid: each version was printed by its own Lean here.
  - Rendering from these parts. No page was drawn from a manifest and blobs. Whether the parts
    are complete for render (citations, suppressed declarations, docstring link resolution)
    is inferred from the IR and render's source, not checked.
  - The hash -> pack lookup of shape (ii) (section 5, premises).
  - Smaller hashes. A 6-byte hash would cut the manifest by about 25%. At 1 M blobs the chance
    of any collision would be about 0.2% (theoretical), and that was not judged.
  - Delta chains for shape (ii) (whole every k versions, deltas between) — a middle shape, not
    computed.
  - Time. Compressing (brotli 11, xz -9e on 538 MB) took about 43 minutes for the first two
    versions and the minor pair. That is bytes-only work, and none of it is a build budget
    number.

  --- u10parts.py
    #!/usr/bin/env python3
    """U10: split one version's IR into the parts of the multi-version design.

    usage: u10parts.py <keep> <outdir> [--limit N]

    Writes into <outdir>:
      content.tsv         hash \t module \t kind \t blob        (every blob of this version, page order)
      content-norefs.tsv  the same, declaration blobs without `refs` (level (e))
      manifest.json       {module: [hash, ...]} in page order
      manifest.bin        per module: name, count, 8-byte hashes
      positions.json      {module: [[line, col, endLine, endCol, selectionRange] | [line, col], ...]}
      positions-rel.json  the same, each item relative to the previous one
      posbyname.tsv       module \t key \t line \t rest-json   (input for the shift-aware delta)
      deplinks.json       {"rev": {root: repo@revision}, "names": {name: [module, startLine, endLine]}}
    """
    import hashlib
    import json
    import os
    import struct
    import sys

    CORE_ROOTS = {"Init", "Lean", "Std", "Lake"}
    PKG_KEY = {"Batteries": "batteries", "Aesop": "aesop", "Qq": "qq", "ProofWidgets": "proofwidgets",
               "Plausible": "plausible", "ImportGraph": "importgraph",
               "LeanSearchClient": "leansearchclient", "Cli": "cli"}
    POS_KEYS = ("line", "col", "endLine", "endCol", "selectionRange")
    DROP = set(POS_KEYS) | {"index"}
    RENAME = ("implicit_reducible", "instance_reducible")
    MODULE_SKIP = {"declarations", "moduleDocs", "schemaVersion"}


    def dumps(x):
        return json.dumps(x, ensure_ascii=False, separators=(",", ":"))


    def hblob(b):
        return hashlib.blake2b(b, digest_size=8).digest()


    def decl_blob(d, with_refs=True):
        out = {}
        for k, v in d.items():
            if k in DROP:
                continue
            if k == "refs" and not with_refs:
                continue
            if k == "attrs":
                v = json.loads(dumps(v).replace(RENAME[0], RENAME[1]))
            out[k] = v
        return dumps(out).encode()


    def span_names(spans, acc):
        for s in spans:
            if len(s) > 3 and isinstance(s[3], str):
                acc.add(s[3])


    def linked_names(d):
        acc = set()
        span_names(d.get("typeCode", []), acc)
        for b in d.get("binderCode", []):
            span_names(b, acc)
        for e in d.get("equationCode", []):
            span_names(e, acc)
        for m in d.get("members", []):
            span_names(m.get("code", []), acc)
            for b in m.get("binderCode", []):
                span_names(b, acc)
        return acc


    def page_items(mod):
        items = []
        seq = 0
        for i, md in enumerate(mod.get("moduleDocs", [])):
            items.append((md.get("line", 0), md.get("col", 0), seq, True, i))
            seq += 1
        for i, d in enumerate(mod["declarations"]):
            items.append((d.get("line", 0), d.get("col", 0), seq + d.get("index", 0), False, i))
        items.sort(key=lambda t: (t[0], t[1], t[2]))
        return items


    def pos_of(d):
        return [d.get(k) for k in POS_KEYS]


    def rel(prev_line, p):
        line, col, end_line, end_col, sel = p
        r = [line - prev_line, col, end_line - line, end_col]
        if sel is not None:
            r.append(sel_rel(sel, line))
        return r


    def sel_rel(sel, line):
        if isinstance(sel, dict):
            sl = sel.get("line", line)
            return [sl - line, sel.get("col"), sel.get("endLine", sl) - sl, sel.get("endCol")]
        if isinstance(sel, list) and len(sel) == 4 and all(isinstance(x, int) for x in sel):
            return [sel[0] - line, sel[1], sel[2] - sel[0], sel[3]]
        return sel


    def main():
        args = sys.argv[1:]
        limit = None
        if "--limit" in args:
            i = args.index("--limit")
            limit = int(args[i + 1])
            del args[i:i + 2]
        keep, outdir = args
        os.makedirs(outdir, exist_ok=True)
        index = json.load(open(os.path.join(keep, "ir", "index.json")))
        lean_rev = open(os.path.join(keep, "lean-githash.txt")).read().strip()
        manifest_pk = json.load(open(os.path.join(keep, "lake-manifest.json")))
        pkg_rev = {p["name"].lower(): p.get("rev", "") for p in manifest_pk["packages"]}
        lidx = {}
        with open(os.path.join(keep, "link-index.lidx"), encoding="utf-8") as f:
            for line in f:
                if line.startswith("\t"):
                    parts = line.rstrip("\n").split("\t")
                    if len(parts) == 4:
                        lidx[parts[1]] = (parts[2], parts[3])
        mods = index["modules"]
        if limit is not None:
            mods = mods[:limit]
        seen = {}
        content = open(os.path.join(outdir, "content.tsv"), "w", encoding="utf-8")
        content_nr = open(os.path.join(outdir, "content-norefs.tsv"), "w", encoding="utf-8")
        posbyname = open(os.path.join(outdir, "posbyname.tsv"), "w", encoding="utf-8")
        manifest = {}
        mbin = bytearray()
        positions = {}
        positions_rel = {}
        dep = {}
        roots_used = set()
        n_decl = n_doc = 0
        for m in mods:
            mod = json.load(open(os.path.join(keep, "ir", m["file"]), encoding="utf-8"))
            name = mod["module"]
            header = {k: v for k, v in mod.items() if k not in MODULE_SKIP}
            hb = dumps(header).encode()
            hashes = []
            poss = []
            prels = []
            prev_line = 0

            def emit(kind, blob, blob_nr):
                h = hblob(blob)
                hx = h.hex()
                if hx in seen:
                    if seen[hx] != hashlib.sha256(blob).digest():
                        raise SystemExit(f"hash collision on {hx}")
                else:
                    seen[hx] = hashlib.sha256(blob).digest()
                content.write(f"{hx}\t{name}\t{kind}\t{blob.decode()}\n")
                content_nr.write(f"{hblob(blob_nr).hex()}\t{name}\t{kind}\t{blob_nr.decode()}\n")
                return h

            hashes.append(emit("H", hb, hb))
            refmod_all = {}
            for it in page_items(mod):
                line, col, _, is_doc, i = it
                if is_doc:
                    md = mod["moduleDocs"][i]
                    b = dumps({"moduleDoc": md.get("text")}).encode()
                    hashes.append(emit("M", b, b))
                    poss.append([md.get("line"), md.get("col")])
                    prels.append([md.get("line") - prev_line, md.get("col")])
                    posbyname.write(f"{name}\tdoc:{hashlib.blake2b(b, digest_size=8).hexdigest()}\t{md.get('line')}\t{dumps([md.get('col')])}\n")
                    prev_line = md.get("line")
                    n_doc += 1
                else:
                    d = mod["declarations"][i]
                    hashes.append(emit("D", decl_blob(d), decl_blob(d, with_refs=False)))
                    p = pos_of(d)
                    poss.append(p)
                    prels.append(rel(prev_line, p))
                    r = rel(p[0], p)
                    posbyname.write(f"{name}\t{d['name']}\t{p[0]}\t{dumps(r[1:])}\n")
                    prev_line = p[0]
                    n_decl += 1
                    refmod = {n: mm for mm, n in d.get("refs", [])}
                    for n in linked_names(d):
                        mm = refmod.get(n)
                        if mm is None:
                            continue
                        root = mm.split(".")[0]
                        if root == "Mathlib":
                            continue
                        roots_used.add(root)
                        rng = lidx.get(n)
                        dep[n] = [mm, int(rng[0]), int(rng[1])] if rng else [mm]
            manifest[name] = [h.hex() for h in hashes]
            nb = name.encode()
            mbin += struct.pack("<H", len(nb)) + nb + struct.pack("<I", len(hashes)) + b"".join(hashes)
            positions[name] = poss
            positions_rel[name] = prels
        revs = {}
        for root in sorted(roots_used):
            if root in CORE_ROOTS:
                revs[root] = "lean4@" + lean_rev
            else:
                key = PKG_KEY.get(root, root.lower())
                revs[root] = key + "@" + pkg_rev.get(key, "?")
        with open(os.path.join(outdir, "manifest.json"), "w", encoding="utf-8") as f:
            f.write(dumps(manifest))
        with open(os.path.join(outdir, "manifest.bin"), "wb") as f:
            f.write(mbin)
        with open(os.path.join(outdir, "positions.json"), "w", encoding="utf-8") as f:
            f.write(dumps(positions))
        with open(os.path.join(outdir, "positions-rel.json"), "w", encoding="utf-8") as f:
            f.write(dumps(positions_rel))
        with open(os.path.join(outdir, "deplinks.json"), "w", encoding="utf-8") as f:
            f.write(dumps({"rev": revs, "names": {k: dep[k] for k in sorted(dep)}}))
        content.close()
        content_nr.close()
        posbyname.close()
        print(dumps({"modules": len(mods), "decls": n_decl, "moduleDocs": n_doc, "distinct_blobs": len(seen),
                     "dep_names": len(dep), "roots": revs}))


    if __name__ == "__main__":
        main()

  --- u10pair.py
    #!/usr/bin/env python3
    """U10: content-store growth and semantic deltas between two versions' parts (u10parts.py output).

    usage: u10pair.py <partsA> <partsB> <outdir>

    Writes into <outdir>:
      new-blobs.tsv / new-blobs-norefs.tsv   blobs of B whose hash is not in A (module \t blob)
      manifest-sem.json    per module: hashes removed / added (by position-free diff) and modules added/removed
      positions-sem.json   shift-aware delta of B's positions against A's
      deplinks-sem.json    names added / removed / changed, revisions changed
    and prints counts as JSON.
    """
    import difflib
    import json
    import os
    import sys


    def dumps(x):
        return json.dumps(x, ensure_ascii=False, separators=(",", ":"))


    def read_hashes(path):
        hs = set()
        with open(path, encoding="utf-8") as f:
            for line in f:
                hs.add(line[:16])
        return hs


    def new_blobs(pa, pb, name, out):
        old = read_hashes(os.path.join(pa, name))
        seen = set()
        n = nb = 0
        kinds = {}
        with open(os.path.join(pb, name), encoding="utf-8") as f, open(out, "w", encoding="utf-8") as o:
            for line in f:
                h, mod, kind, blob = line.rstrip("\n").split("\t", 3)
                if h in old or h in seen:
                    continue
                seen.add(h)
                o.write(f"{mod}\t{blob}\n")
                n += 1
                nb += len(blob.encode())
                kinds[kind] = kinds.get(kind, 0) + 1
        return {"new_blobs": n, "new_blob_bytes": nb, "by_kind": kinds}


    def manifest_sem(pa, pb):
        A = json.load(open(os.path.join(pa, "manifest.json")))
        B = json.load(open(os.path.join(pb, "manifest.json")))
        delta = {"removed_modules": sorted(set(A) - set(B)), "modules": {}}
        st = {"modules_added": 0, "modules_removed": len(set(A) - set(B)), "modules_changed": 0,
              "entries_added": 0, "entries_removed": 0, "entries_B": 0, "entries_same_position": 0}
        for m, hb in B.items():
            st["entries_B"] += len(hb)
            ha = A.get(m)
            if ha is None:
                st["modules_added"] += 1
                st["entries_added"] += len(hb)
                delta["modules"][m] = {"new": hb}
                continue
            if ha == hb:
                st["entries_same_position"] += len(hb)
                continue
            st["modules_changed"] += 1
            ops = []
            sm = difflib.SequenceMatcher(a=ha, b=hb, autojunk=False)
            for tag, i1, i2, j1, j2 in sm.get_opcodes():
                if tag == "equal":
                    st["entries_same_position"] += i2 - i1
                    continue
                st["entries_removed"] += i2 - i1
                st["entries_added"] += j2 - j1
                ops.append([i1, i2 - i1, hb[j1:j2]])
            delta["modules"][m] = {"ops": ops}
        return delta, st


    def read_pos(path):
        out = {}
        occ = {}
        with open(path, encoding="utf-8") as f:
            for line in f:
                mod, key, ln, rest = line.rstrip("\n").split("\t")
                n = occ.get((mod, key), 0)
                occ[(mod, key)] = n + 1
                out.setdefault(mod, []).append((f"{key}#{n}" if n else key, int(ln), rest))
        return out


    def positions_sem(pa, pb):
        A = read_pos(os.path.join(pa, "posbyname.tsv"))
        B = read_pos(os.path.join(pb, "posbyname.tsv"))
        delta = {}
        st = {"items_B": 0, "same_position": 0, "shifted_same_shape": 0, "explicit": 0, "runs": 0,
              "modules_unchanged": 0}
        for m, items in B.items():
            st["items_B"] += len(items)
            old = {k: (ln, rest) for k, ln, rest in A.get(m, [])}
            runs = []
            explicit = []
            cur = None
            unchanged = True
            for idx, (k, ln, rest) in enumerate(items):
                o = old.get(k)
                if o is not None and o[1] == rest:
                    shift = ln - o[0]
                    if shift == 0:
                        st["same_position"] += 1
                    else:
                        st["shifted_same_shape"] += 1
                        unchanged = False
                    if cur is not None and cur[2] == shift and cur[0] + cur[1] == idx:
                        cur[1] += 1
                    else:
                        cur = [idx, 1, shift]
                        runs.append(cur)
                else:
                    unchanged = False
                    st["explicit"] += 1
                    explicit.append([idx, ln, json.loads(rest)])
                    cur = None
            if unchanged and len(A.get(m, [])) == len(items):
                st["modules_unchanged"] += 1
                continue
            st["runs"] += len(runs)
            delta[m] = {"runs": runs, "explicit": explicit}
        st["modules_removed"] = len(set(A) - set(B))
        return delta, st


    def deplinks_sem(pa, pb):
        A = json.load(open(os.path.join(pa, "deplinks.json")))
        B = json.load(open(os.path.join(pb, "deplinks.json")))
        na, nb = A["names"], B["names"]
        delta = {"rev": {k: v for k, v in B["rev"].items() if A["rev"].get(k) != v},
                 "removed": sorted(set(na) - set(nb)),
                 "set": {k: v for k, v in nb.items() if na.get(k) != v}}
        st = {"names_A": len(na), "names_B": len(nb), "added": len(set(nb) - set(na)),
              "removed": len(set(na) - set(nb)),
              "changed": sum(1 for k in set(na) & set(nb) if na[k] != nb[k]),
              "revs_changed": len(delta["rev"])}
        return delta, st


    def main():
        pa, pb, outdir = sys.argv[1:]
        os.makedirs(outdir, exist_ok=True)
        rep = {}
        rep["content"] = new_blobs(pa, pb, "content.tsv", os.path.join(outdir, "new-blobs.tsv"))
        rep["content_norefs"] = new_blobs(pa, pb, "content-norefs.tsv", os.path.join(outdir, "new-blobs-norefs.tsv"))
        d, rep["manifest"] = manifest_sem(pa, pb)
        open(os.path.join(outdir, "manifest-sem.json"), "w").write(dumps(d))
        d, rep["positions"] = positions_sem(pa, pb)
        open(os.path.join(outdir, "positions-sem.json"), "w").write(dumps(d))
        d, rep["deplinks"] = deplinks_sem(pa, pb)
        open(os.path.join(outdir, "deplinks-sem.json"), "w").write(dumps(d))
        json.dump(rep, open(os.path.join(outdir, "pair.json"), "w"), indent=1)
        print(json.dumps(rep, indent=1))


    if __name__ == "__main__":
        main()

  --- u10measure.py
    #!/usr/bin/env python3
    """U10: sizes of every part, whole and as deltas.

    usage: u10measure.py <root> <out.json> [--versions a,b,c] [--skip-br-packs]
    <root> holds parts/<tag>/ (u10parts.py), pairs/<A>_<B>/ (u10pair.py) and keep/<tag>/site/.
    """
    import gzip
    import json
    import os
    import subprocess
    import sys
    import zlib

    GLOBALS = ["declarations/used-by.json", "declarations/name-map.json", "search-index.bin",
               "instances.json", "modules.json", "index.html", "404.html", "search.html",
               "foundational_types.html", "references.html"]


    def gz6(b):
        return len(gzip.compress(b, compresslevel=6, mtime=0))


    def deflate6(b):
        c = zlib.compressobj(6, zlib.DEFLATED, -15)
        return len(c.compress(b) + c.flush())


    def cli(cmd, data=None):
        r = subprocess.run(cmd, input=data, stdout=subprocess.PIPE, stderr=subprocess.PIPE)
        if r.returncode != 0:
            raise SystemExit(f"{cmd} failed: {r.stderr[:500]!r}")
        return r.stdout


    def fsize(p):
        return os.stat(p).st_size


    def file_sizes(path, tmp):
        raw = fsize(path)
        return {"raw": raw,
                "gz6": len(cli(["gzip", "-6", "-n", "-c", path])),
                "br11": len(cli(["brotli", "-q", "11", "-c", path])),
                "zstd19": len(cli(["zstd", "-19", "--long=31", "-q", "-c", path]))}


    def patch(old, new, tmp, check=False):
        out = os.path.join(tmp, "patch.zst")
        cli(["zstd", "-19", "--long=31", "-q", "-f", "--patch-from=" + old, new, "-o", out])
        n = fsize(out)
        if check:
            back = os.path.join(tmp, "patch.back")
            cli(["zstd", "-d", "--long=31", "-q", "-f", "--patch-from=" + old, out, "-o", back])
            if open(back, "rb").read() != open(new, "rb").read():
                raise SystemExit(f"patch round trip failed for {new}")
            os.remove(back)
        os.remove(out)
        return n


    def stream_sizes(data, tmp, name):
        p = os.path.join(tmp, name)
        open(p, "wb").write(data)
        r = {"raw": len(data), "gz6": len(cli(["gzip", "-6", "-n", "-c", p])),
             "zstd19": len(cli(["zstd", "-19", "--long=31", "-q", "-c", p])),
             "xz9e": len(cli(["xz", "-9e", "-c", p]))}
        os.remove(p)
        return r


    def packs(lines, br=False, tmp=None):
        """lines: iterable of (module, blob str). One pack per module, blobs joined with newline."""
        by_mod = {}
        for mod, blob in lines:
            by_mod.setdefault(mod, []).append(blob)
        tot_gz = tot_raw = tot_br = 0
        largest_gz = 0
        for mod, blobs in by_mod.items():
            b = "\n".join(blobs).encode()
            tot_raw += len(b)
            g = gz6(b)
            tot_gz += g
            largest_gz = max(largest_gz, g)
            if br:
                tot_br += len(cli(["brotli", "-q", "11", "-c"], b))
        r = {"packs": len(by_mod), "raw": tot_raw, "gz6": tot_gz, "largest_gz6": largest_gz}
        if br:
            r["br11"] = tot_br
        return r


    def per_blob(blobs):
        g = d = 0
        for b in blobs:
            g += gz6(b)
            d += deflate6(b)
        return {"gz6_each": g, "deflate6_each": d}


    def distinct_content(path):
        seen = set()
        out = []
        with open(path, encoding="utf-8") as f:
            for line in f:
                h, mod, kind, blob = line.rstrip("\n").split("\t", 3)
                if h in seen:
                    continue
                seen.add(h)
                out.append((mod, blob))
        return out


    def content_whole(parts, tmp, name, br):
        items = distinct_content(os.path.join(parts, name))
        blobs = [b.encode() for _, b in items]
        r = {"blobs": len(blobs), "raw": sum(len(b) for b in blobs)}
        r.update(per_blob(blobs))
        r["per_module"] = packs(items, br=br)
        r["one_stream"] = stream_sizes(b"\n".join(blobs), tmp, "all.blobs")
        return r


    def content_count(parts, name):
        items = distinct_content(os.path.join(parts, name))
        return {"blobs": len(items), "raw": sum(len(b.encode()) for _, b in items)}


    def content_new(pair, tmp, name, old_stream_path):
        items = []
        with open(os.path.join(pair, name), encoding="utf-8") as f:
            for line in f:
                mod, blob = line.rstrip("\n").split("\t", 1)
                items.append((mod, blob))
        blobs = [b.encode() for _, b in items]
        r = {"blobs": len(blobs), "raw": sum(len(b) for b in blobs)}
        r.update(per_blob(blobs))
        r["per_module"] = packs(items, br=True)
        data = b"\n".join(blobs)
        r["one_stream"] = stream_sizes(data, tmp, "new.blobs")
        if old_stream_path is not None:
            p = os.path.join(tmp, "new.blobs")
            open(p, "wb").write(data)
            r["one_stream_patch_from_old_store"] = patch(old_stream_path, p, tmp)
            os.remove(p)
        return r


    def write_store(parts, name, path):
        items = distinct_content(os.path.join(parts, name))
        open(path, "wb").write(b"\n".join(b.encode() for _, b in items))


    def usedby_sem(old, new, tmp):
        A = json.load(open(old, encoding="utf-8"))
        B = json.load(open(new, encoding="utf-8"))
        pa = {(k, u) for k, us in A.items() for u in us}
        pb = {(k, u) for k, us in B.items() for u in us}
        added = sorted(pb - pa)
        removed = sorted(pa - pb)
        text = "".join(f"+\t{k}\t{u}\n" for k, u in added) + "".join(f"-\t{k}\t{u}\n" for k, u in removed)
        r = {"pairs_A": len(pa), "pairs_B": len(pb), "added": len(added), "removed": len(removed),
             "targets_A": len(A), "targets_B": len(B),
             "targets_changed": sum(1 for k in set(A) | set(B) if set(A.get(k, [])) != set(B.get(k, [])))}
        r["delta_text"] = stream_sizes(text.encode(), tmp, "ub.delta")
        return r
