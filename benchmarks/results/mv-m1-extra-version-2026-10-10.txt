M1, per extra version: one minor-pair round of the reader session over all of Mathlib
======================================================================================

The "M1, per extra version" measurement of docs/multiversion/implementation.md ("Item 6 plan",
Measurement), 2026-10-10. Workload: the U9 pair, Mathlib v4.31.0 (R0) -> v4.32.0 (R1), read into
v4.34.1, in one `reader session` per process. Five processes of R0 + R1; R1 is the figure. One more
process with --check-patch --check-keys, and one `reader extract` of v4.32.0 alone as the oracle.
